Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

In short: Stickio stores your notes locally on your computer. The application does not create an account and does not upload note text to a server.

Stickio application

Stickio stores notes, settings, and local backups on your Windows computer. The app does not need an internet connection for its main functions and does not send your notes, search text, or note database to us.

Website data

The website may receive technical request data that a web server normally needs to operate, such as IP address, browser type, requested page and time of the request. This information is used for security, diagnostics and delivery of the website.

Downloads

When you download the installer, the server may record technical download information in its access log. This does not include the contents of your notes or any data from the installed application.

Third parties

The site does not require registration. We do not sell personal data. If web analytics is added in the future, this policy will be updated before it is used.

Contact

For questions about privacy, use the project page at github.com/strimer86/Stickio.
