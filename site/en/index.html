Common questions

FAQ

Is Stickio really free?

Yes. The entire program is free: no paid edition, subscription, ads, registration or account.

Where are my notes stored?

Only on your computer, in a local Stickio database. Notes are never uploaded to a cloud service.

Will notes disappear after restarting?

No. Text is saved automatically while you type and remains after closing the app or restarting Windows.

Are administrator rights needed?

No. Choose installation for the current user in the installer to install Stickio without administrator rights.

Can I move notes to another PC?

Yes. Use the tray menu to export notes to JSON or HTML and import them on another computer.

Which interface languages are available?

Stickio is available in English and Russian. You can switch the language in Settings.
